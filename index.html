Is it time to put snow tires on?

Why 45°F?

When should you put snow tires on?

The usual rule of thumb is to switch to snow (winter) tires once average daily temperatures stay at or below about 45°F (7°C). Below that point, the rubber compound in all-season and summer tires hardens and loses grip, even on dry pavement. Winter tires stay soft in the cold, which helps with braking and handling on ice, slush and snow.

This site checks the National Weather Service 7-day forecast for your ZIP code or current location, averages the forecast temperatures, and looks for snow in the forecast. If the average is 45°F or colder, it tells you to get those snow tires on.

When should you take them off?

Winter tires wear quickly in warm weather, so most people swap back to all-seasons once temperatures are consistently above 45°F. You can use this same check in the spring.
